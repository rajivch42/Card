# Themes and Image Briefs (Uttar Pradesh)

Five ready-made looks. All share the same page structure (see `prd.md`); only tokens, ornaments, illustrations and animation flavour change. Set one value, `theme`, in the config.

Use the sample invitations you found as inspiration for **layout ideas only** (arch frames, venue paintings, monogram, scroll opening). Their artwork belongs to their designers, so commission or generate your own original illustrations.

## What the samples teach us
| Sample | Pattern worth borrowing |
|---|---|
| Green arch card with pink arcades and fountains, formal "The [Family] request the pleasure of your gracious presence..." wording | A tall arch as the main frame; garden illustration at the bottom; family-hosted opening line; petals drifting through the scene |
| Cream parchment scroll with lotus border and a Hindi monogram on a purple background, hanging diyas | An opening "unrolling scroll" moment; a couple monogram as the invitation's logo |
| Terracotta scalloped arch card with parrot icon, "The 7 steps to forever", date block "FEB / 3 / 2026" | A clean date block (month, large numeral, year between thin lines); a small bird or motif above the title; wildflower border |
| Reception card on a phone with a watercolor painting of the venue and a round mute button | A hand-painted venue scene on each event card; the mute button staying visible |
| Red theatre card with drapes, arched alcoves and musicians | Event-specific scenes (a Sangeet can be playful and theatrical) |

## Theme A: Awadhi Royal (default)
Nawabi elegance: jharokha arches, chikankari-style white embroidery lines, gold filigree.
- **Colors:** ivory `#FBF5EA`, maroon `#6E1F2E`, gold `#C9A24B`, emerald `#1F5B4A`, blush `#E9C7C0`
- **Ornaments:** scalloped arch frame, chikankari border strip, small paan-leaf and lotus motifs, a skyline silhouette of a grand imambara-style gateway at the bottom
- **Opening animation:** two carved wooden doors open outward to reveal the hero card
- **Hero background:** ivory with faint gold jali pattern; marigold and rose garlands hang from the top and sway
- **Particles:** rose petals and tiny gold sparkles
- **Fonts:** Pinyon Script (names), Cormorant Garamond (body), Tiro Devanagari Hindi (Hindi)

## Theme B: Kashi Aarti
Evening on the ghats: diyas, marigold, temple spires, river glow.
- **Colors:** deep indigo `#1B1B3A`, saffron `#F29A2E`, marigold `#F5B82E`, cream `#FFF4DC`, vermilion `#C8442F`
- **Ornaments:** stepped ghat silhouette, floating diyas on a rippling river, marigold toran across the top
- **Opening animation:** a single diya lights, flame spreads to a row of diyas, and the screen brightens into the invitation
- **Particles:** marigold petals and rising sparks
- **Fonts:** Great Vibes (names), Cormorant Garamond, Tiro Devanagari Hindi

## Theme C: Mughal Garden
Marble and gardens: arches, fountains, pietra-dura flowers, soft pink and sage.
- **Colors:** marble white `#F7F3EE`, rose `#E4A3AE`, sage `#6F8F72`, deep green `#2F4F3E`, gold `#C9A24B`
- **Ornaments:** tall arch frame with symmetrical arcades, fountain and cypress garden at the bottom, inlaid floral border
- **Opening animation:** the arch opens like a gate, a soft zoom through it into the garden
- **Particles:** pink petals with gentle sway
- **Fonts:** Pinyon Script, Cormorant Garamond, Noto Serif Devanagari

## Theme D: Braj Peacock
Peacock feathers, lotus ponds and haveli courtyards.
- **Colors:** peacock teal `#0F5E63`, deep green `#16453F`, gold `#D2A94A`, cream `#FFF8E7`, magenta accent `#B83A6B`
- **Ornaments:** peacock feather corner flourishes, lotus pond, haveli jharokha windows with lanterns
- **Opening animation:** a peacock feather fan opens from the centre
- **Particles:** feather-shaped drifting petals and fireflies
- **Fonts:** Kaushan Script (headings), Cormorant Garamond, Tiro Devanagari Hindi

## Theme E: Royal Scroll
A parchment scroll, a lotus wreath and a couple monogram.
- **Colors:** parchment `#F6EBD2`, royal purple `#3E1A5A`, lotus pink `#C94F7C`, gold `#C9A24B`
- **Ornaments:** rope-tied scroll, hanging brass diyas at the corners, monogram in a lotus wreath
- **Opening animation:** the scroll unrolls downward, monogram appears, then the names write in
- **Particles:** gold dust and lotus petals
- **Fonts:** Pinyon Script, Cormorant Garamond, Noto Serif Devanagari

## Image briefs (for an illustrator or image generator)
Format: portrait 1080 x 1920, delivered as WebP. Keep the top 30% and bottom 20% calmer so text can sit over them.
1. **Hero floral background:** watercolor roses and marigold along the top, fading to cream. Original artwork, no logos.
2. **Venue painting (one per venue):** a hand-painted view of your real venue in {{CITY}}, Uttar Pradesh, evening light, warm colors. Ask the venue for reference photos.
3. **Couple illustration:** two figures in traditional dress, faces simple and stylised; match the couple's real outfits if possible.
4. **Event cards:** one per enabled event, matching the table above, with space for the event name, date block and time.
5. **Ornament set (SVG):** arch frame, scalloped border, chikankari strip, jali pattern tile, marigold garland, diya, kalash, lotus, peacock feather, small Ganesha line icon.
6. **Monogram (SVG):** initials of the couple in a lotus or floral wreath.
7. **Open Graph image:** 1200 x 630 with names, date and city for WhatsApp previews.

## Wording for UP guests
- Offer a language switch: English and Hindi (हिंदी).
- Use family-hosted opening text, for example: "{{HOST_FAMILY}} request the pleasure of your gracious presence at the wedding of..."
- Event time formats: "4:00 PM onwards" style, with the day name.
- Add a travel note: nearest railway station, airport, local contact number, and parking.
